Buzz test — name that tune

The whole round on one page, using your own Spotify: a song plays, you buzz to stop it, then you say what it is. No room, nobody else, nothing to set up — it borrows the Spotify login from the main game, so if you are connected there you are connected here.

What to try

Play it, buzz as soon as you know it, then say the song and the artist. Both scores double, one of the two scores half. Try it sloppily too — "erm, is it Wonderwall by Oasis?" should still score.

If it marks you wrong, the grey box shows exactly what your phone heard and how close it got — that separates the phone mishearing you from my marking being too strict, which need opposite fixes.
